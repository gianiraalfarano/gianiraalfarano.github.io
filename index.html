ABOUT

Since September 2024, I am Chaire de Professeure Junior (tenure-track position) in the Géométrie et Algèbre Effectives team at Rennes University.
I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematics under the supervision Prof. Dr. Joachim Rosenthal at the University of Zurich, on June 2022. Then, I was part of the Mathematical Communication Theory group at TU Eindhoven, led by Prof. Dr. Alberto Ravagnani, from October 2022 to September 2023, and I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Applied Algebra and Information Theory group at University College Dublin (UCD), working in the team of Prof. Dr. Eimear Byrne from October 2023 to August 2024.
My research interests include Algebraic Coding Theory, Finite Fields, Combinatorics, Finite Geometry.

I strongly believe in Federico Ardila-Mantilla's Axioms:

  • Axiom 1. Mathematical talent is distributed equally among different groups, irrespective of geographic, demographic, and economic boundaries.
  • Axiom 2. Everyone can have joyful, meaningful, and empowering mathematical experiences.
  • Axiom 3. Mathematics is a powerful, malleable tool that can be shaped and used differently by various communities to serve their needs.
  • Axiom 4. Every student deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.
